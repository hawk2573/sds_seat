ⓘ Drag & drop — click and hold an occupied seat, then drop it on any other seat. Dropping on an empty seat moves the guest; dropping on another occupied seat swaps the two guests. Both records are updated in Airtable.
ⓘ Avatars — when a guest has no Photo attachment, a colored circle with their initials is shown as a placeholder.
ⓘ Click an occupied seat (optional for v1) — a small popover can display meal preference, notes, and email.
